# Deposit Interest Rate Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes Meridian Small Finance Bank Ltd.'s framework for
setting and revising interest rates on deposit products, in compliance with
the Reserve Bank of India's directions applicable to Small Finance Banks
regarding interest rates on deposits.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all rupee and foreign-currency deposit products
offered by the Bank, including savings deposits, term deposits, Non-Resident
External (NRE) Rupee deposits, and Foreign Currency Non-Resident (Bank)
[FCNR(B)] deposits.

## 3. General Interest Rate Ceiling Provisions

3.1. The Bank's Asset Liability Committee (ALCO) shall determine interest
rates on deposits within the ceilings prescribed by the Reserve Bank of
India's Master Direction on Interest Rate on Deposits applicable to Small
Finance Banks, as amended from time to time.

3.2. Interest rates once fixed for a given tenor shall not be changed for
deposits already accepted, except where the Bank has offered a floating-rate
deposit product with explicit terms permitting such variation.

## 4. Temporary Relaxation — FCNR(B) and NRE Term Deposits

4.1. Pursuant to RBI's temporary relaxation of the interest rate ceiling
applicable to certain long-tenor foreign-currency and NRE deposits, the Bank
may offer rates above the standard ceiling on:

  a. Fresh FCNR(B) deposits of 3-5 year tenors; and
  b. Fresh NRE term deposits of 3 years and above tenor, including deposits
     renewed upon maturity.

4.2. This temporary relaxation is effective from June 17, 2026, and remains
available **for the period until September 30, 2026**, in accordance with
RBI's direction dated November 28, 2025, as most recently updated.

4.3. The Bank's branches and digital deposit channels shall be configured to
automatically discontinue offering the relaxed rate on deposits booked after
the applicable end date stated in Section 4.2, reverting to the standard
ceiling thereafter.

## 5. Review and Escalation

5.1. This policy shall be reviewed whenever RBI issues an amendment to the
applicable Master Direction on Interest Rate on Deposits, or at least
annually, whichever is earlier.

5.2. The Compliance function is responsible for monitoring RBI notifications
and circulars relevant to this policy and escalating any required amendment
to ALCO within 5 business days of publication.

## 6. Related Regulatory References

- Reserve Bank of India (Small Finance Banks – Interest Rate on Deposits)
  Directions, 2025, dated November 28, 2025 (as updated June 17, 2026)
