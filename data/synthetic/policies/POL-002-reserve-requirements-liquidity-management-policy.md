# Reserve Requirements & Liquidity Management Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out Meridian Small Finance Bank Ltd.'s framework for
maintaining the Cash Reserve Ratio (CRR) and Statutory Liquidity Ratio (SLR)
as required under the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 and the Banking
Regulation Act, 1949, and RBI's Master Direction on CRR and SLR applicable
to Small Finance Banks.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to the Bank's Treasury function and covers all Net
Demand and Time Liabilities (NDTL) subject to CRR and SLR maintenance
requirements.

## 3. Standard CRR/SLR Maintenance

3.1. The Bank shall maintain CRR and SLR at the levels prescribed by RBI
from time to time, computed on NDTL as reported in the relevant fortnightly
return.

3.2. The Treasury function shall monitor daily CRR maintenance against the
fortnightly average requirement and escalate any projected shortfall to the
Chief Financial Officer immediately.

## 4. Temporary Exemption — FCNR(B) and NRE Deposits

4.1. In accordance with RBI's exemption from CRR and SLR maintenance for
certain incremental foreign-currency and NRE deposits, the Bank shall
exclude from its NDTL base for CRR/SLR purposes:

  a. Fresh FCNR(B) deposits of minimum 3-year and maximum 5-year tenor
     mobilized between June 8, 2026 and **September 30, 2026**; and
  b. Fresh NRE term deposits of 3 years or more tenor (including deposits
     renewed upon maturity) mobilized between June 19, 2026 and
     **September 30, 2026**.

4.2. Deposits mobilized after the applicable end dates in Section 4.1 shall
be included in the NDTL base for CRR/SLR computation in the ordinary course.

4.3. The Treasury Operations team shall maintain a tagged ledger of deposits
claimed under this exemption to support any RBI supervisory review.

## 5. Review and Escalation

5.1. This policy shall be reviewed whenever RBI issues an amendment to the
applicable Master Direction on CRR and SLR, or at least annually.

5.2. The Compliance function shall notify Treasury of any change to the
exemption period, exemption scope, or underlying statutory provisions
within 5 business days of an RBI notification.

## 6. Related Regulatory References
