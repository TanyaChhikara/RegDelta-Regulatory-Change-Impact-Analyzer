# Fraud Risk Management Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes Meridian Small Finance Bank Ltd.'s Board-approved
framework for fraud risk management, in accordance with RBI's directions on
fraud risk management for regulated entities.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all business lines, products, and channels of the
Bank, and covers internal fraud (by employees), external fraud (by
customers or third parties), and digital/cyber-enabled fraud.

## 3. Fraud Risk Governance

3.1. The Board's Risk Management Committee has oversight of fraud risk
management, supported by a designated Chief Fraud Risk Officer.

3.2. A Fraud Risk Management Group (FRMG) shall be responsible for fraud
detection, investigation, and reporting.

## 4. Detection and Early Warning

4.1. The Bank shall maintain a transaction monitoring system capable of
generating alerts based on defined fraud risk indicators, and shall classify
accounts under Early Warning Signals (EWS) categories as prescribed by RBI
where applicable.

4.2. Any transaction or account flagged as a suspected fraud shall be
escalated to the FRMG within 24 hours of detection.

## 5. Fraud Classification and Reporting

5.1. Confirmed fraud cases shall be classified in accordance with RBI's
prescribed fraud categories and reported via the applicable RBI reporting
system (e.g. the Central Fraud Registry) within prescribed timelines.

5.2. Fraud cases involving amounts above the Board-approved threshold shall
be reported to the Audit Committee of the Board.

## 6. Review and Escalation

6.1. This policy shall be reviewed annually, or upon any amendment to RBI's
directions on fraud risk management for regulated entities.

## 7. Related Regulatory References

- Reserve Bank of India directions on Fraud Risk Management for regulated
  entities, as applicable and amended from time to time
