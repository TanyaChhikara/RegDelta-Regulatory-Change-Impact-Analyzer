# KYC and Customer Due Diligence Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out Meridian Small Finance Bank Ltd.'s Know Your Customer
(KYC) and Customer Due Diligence (CDD) framework, in compliance with RBI's
Master Direction on KYC and the Prevention of Money Laundering Act, 2002
(PMLA) and rules made thereunder.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to onboarding and ongoing due diligence for all
individual, sole proprietorship, partnership, and corporate customers of the
Bank across all channels.

## 3. Customer Identification Procedure (CIP)

3.1. The Bank shall obtain Officially Valid Documents (OVDs) for identity
and address verification at the time of account opening, in accordance with
the current list of OVDs prescribed under the Master Direction on KYC.

3.2. Aadhaar-based e-KYC and Video-based Customer Identification Process
(V-CIP) shall be conducted in accordance with RBI's prescribed procedure and
technical standards.

## 4. Risk Categorization

4.1. Customers shall be categorized as low, medium, or high risk based on
factors including nature of business, geographic location, and transaction
profile, with periodic re-KYC intervals of 10, 8, and 2 years respectively.

4.2. Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs) shall be subject to enhanced due
diligence and senior management approval prior to account opening.

## 5. Ongoing Monitoring

5.1. The Bank shall monitor customer transactions on an ongoing basis for
consistency with the customer's declared profile and risk category.

5.2. Suspicious transactions shall be reported to the Financial Intelligence
Unit-India (FIU-IND) in accordance with PMLA reporting requirements.

## 6. Review and Escalation

6.1. This policy shall be reviewed annually, or upon any amendment to RBI's
Master Direction on KYC or applicable PMLA rules.

## 7. Related Regulatory References

- Reserve Bank of India Master Direction on Know Your Customer (KYC)
  Direction, 2016, as amended
