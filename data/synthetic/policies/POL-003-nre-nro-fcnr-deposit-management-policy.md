# NRE/NRO/FCNR(B) Deposit Management Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy governs the Bank's acceptance, servicing, and repatriation of
Non-Resident External (NRE), Non-Resident Ordinary (NRO), and Foreign
Currency Non-Resident (Bank) [FCNR(B)] deposit accounts, in compliance with
FEMA regulations and applicable RBI directions.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to the International Banking Division and all branches
authorized to accept NRE, NRO, and FCNR(B) deposits.

## 3. Standard Terms

3.1. NRE and FCNR(B) deposits shall be accepted only from persons resident
outside India, as defined under FEMA, 1999.

3.2. Interest rates on NRE and FCNR(B) deposits shall not exceed the
ceilings prescribed under the Bank's Deposit Interest Rate Policy (POL-001)
and applicable RBI directions.

## 4. Temporary Relaxation Provisions

4.1. This Division shall apply the temporary interest-rate ceiling
relaxation and the corresponding CRR/SLR exemption (per POL-001 Section 4
and POL-002 Section 4 respectively) to qualifying FCNR(B) deposits of
3-5 year tenor and NRE term deposits of 3 years or more, for deposits booked
during the applicable relaxation window.

4.2. The applicable relaxation window for interest-rate purposes runs from
June 17, 2026 **through September 30, 2026**; the applicable window for the
CRR/SLR exemption runs from June 8, 2026 (FCNR(B)) / June 19, 2026 (NRE)
**through September 30, 2026**, per POL-001 and POL-002 respectively.

4.3. Relationship managers shall inform prospective depositors that rates
and exemption treatment offered under this relaxation apply only to deposits
booked within the applicable window, and that no assurance can be given
regarding availability of the relaxation beyond that date absent a further
RBI extension.

## 5. Repatriation and Reporting

5.1. Repatriation of NRE and FCNR(B) principal and interest shall be
processed in accordance with FEMA regulations and the Bank's standard
foreign exchange remittance procedures.

5.2. NRO account transactions remain subject to the applicable repatriation
limits and require submission of Form 15CA/15CB where applicable.

## 6. Review and Escalation

6.1. This policy is reviewed jointly with POL-001 and POL-002 whenever RBI
amends the underlying interest rate or CRR/SLR directions affecting the
relaxation windows referenced in Section 4.
