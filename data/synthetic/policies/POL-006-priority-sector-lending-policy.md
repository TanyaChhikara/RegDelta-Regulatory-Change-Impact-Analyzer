# Priority Sector Lending Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out Meridian Small Finance Bank Ltd.'s framework for
meeting Priority Sector Lending (PSL) targets prescribed by RBI for Small
Finance Banks.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to the Credit Policy Department and all lending
business units originating loans classified under PSL categories.

## 3. PSL Targets

3.1. The Bank shall maintain overall PSL lending of not less than 75% of
Adjusted Net Bank Credit (ANBC), computed in accordance with RBI's Master
Direction on Priority Sector Lending applicable to Small Finance Banks.

3.2. Sub-targets for Agriculture, Micro Enterprises, and Weaker Sections
shall be maintained in accordance with the applicable Master Direction.

## 4. Eligible Categories

4.1. Eligible PSL categories include Agriculture, Micro, Small and Medium
Enterprises (MSME), Export Credit, Education, Housing, Social Infrastructure,
Renewable Energy, and lending to Weaker Sections, as defined under the
applicable Master Direction.

4.2. Priority Sector Lending Certificates (PSLCs) may be bought or sold by
the Bank to manage PSL target compliance, subject to RBI's PSLC framework.

## 5. Monitoring and Reporting

5.1. PSL achievement shall be monitored quarterly against annual targets and
reported to the Board's Credit Committee.

5.2. Shortfall in PSL targets, if any, shall be assessed for contribution to
the Rural Infrastructure Development Fund (RIDF) or equivalent funds as
prescribed by RBI.

## 6. Review and Escalation

6.1. This policy shall be reviewed annually, or upon any amendment to RBI's
Master Direction on Priority Sector Lending.

## 7. Related Regulatory References

  Targets and Classification, as applicable to Small Finance Banks
